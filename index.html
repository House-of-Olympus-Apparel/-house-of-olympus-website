Customer Care

Return Policy

We want you to love your purchase. If something isn’t quite right, we’re happy to help.

Returns

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery.

Items must be unworn and unwashed, free of stains, odors, makeup, pet hair, or other signs of wear, and in original condition with tags attached when applicable.

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item was damaged, defective, or sent incorrectly.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, your refund will be issued to the original payment method. Please allow your financial institution additional time to process the refund.

Original shipping charges are non-refundable.

Sale & Final Sale

Items marked Final Sale are not eligible for returns or exchanges.

Sale items not specifically marked Final Sale may be returned under our standard return policy.

All sales are final after 14 days.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or you receive the wrong item, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. We’ll make it right.

Exchanges

Because inventory can change quickly, we cannot guarantee the same item or size will still be available. Return the original item and place a new order for the replacement you want.

Questions?

Please contact House of Olympus Apparel before sending your item back.

Customer Questions

FAQ

How long do I have to request a return?

Standard returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery, subject to the return-policy condition requirements.

What condition must a return be in?

Items must be unworn, unwashed, in original condition, and free of stains, odors, makeup, pet hair, or other signs of wear. Tags should remain attached when applicable.

Who pays return shipping?

Customers are responsible for return shipping unless an item was damaged, defective, or sent incorrectly.

Can I return a Final Sale item?

No. Items specifically marked Final Sale are not eligible for return or exchange.

What if my order is damaged or incorrect?

Contact us within 7 days of delivery and include photos of the item and packaging.

How do I contact House of Olympus Apparel?

Email house.of.olympus.apparel@outlook.com.
