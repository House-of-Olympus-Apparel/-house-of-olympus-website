Customer Care

Return Policy

We want you to love your purchase. If something isn’t quite right, we’re happy to help.

Returns

Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed and in original condition with tags attached when applicable.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Original shipping charges are non-refundable.

Final Sale

Items marked Final Sale are not eligible for return or exchange. All sales are final after 14 days.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

Contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the item and packaging.

Customer Questions

FAQ

How long do I have to request a return?

Standard returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery.

Who pays return shipping?

Customers are responsible for return shipping unless an item was damaged, defective or sent incorrectly.

Can I return a Final Sale item?

No. Items specifically marked Final Sale are not eligible for return or exchange.
