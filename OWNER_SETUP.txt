This version preserves the exact v12 visible design.

Owner editing is added separately at /admin/.
Until you add products, the website looks exactly like v12.
When products are added, they replace the placeholder cards in their selected section.

One-time setup still required:
1. Connect Netlify site to GitHub.
2. Enable Netlify Identity.
3. Set registration to Invite only.
4. Enable Git Gateway.
5. Invite your owner email.
6. Visit https://quiet-mochi-33b24c.netlify.app/admin/
